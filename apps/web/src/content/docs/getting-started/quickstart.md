## Step 1: Add Your Contacts

Navigate to **Audience > Contacts** in your dashboard. You can add individual contacts manually or click **Import Contacts** to upload a CSV file. Growixa automatically validates email formats and maps custom fields like First Name, Company, and Phone.

## Step 2: Connect a Sender Identity

Go to **Settings > Integrations**. Connect a **Postmark** API token or your **Custom SMTP / Amazon SES** credentials. Ensure you have added your verified sender email (e.g. `newsletter@yourcompany.com`).

## Step 3: Build & Launch Your Campaign

Head to **Campaigns > + New Campaign** to launch the 5-step campaign wizard:

1. **Setup**: Name your campaign and write a compelling subject line (or click **'✨ Ask AI'** for subject ideas).
2. **Audience**: Select 'All Contacts', a static list, or a dynamic segment.
3. **Template**: Choose a pre-designed responsive template or write custom HTML/Markdown.
4. **AI Review**: Tune the tone, length, and call-to-action.
5. **Review & Send**: Choose **Send Now** for immediate delivery or **Schedule** for a future date and time.
