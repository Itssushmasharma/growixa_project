## Configuring Postmark

1. Log into your Postmark account and copy your **Server API Token**.
2. In Growixa, navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Postmark**.
3. Paste the token and specify your default Broadcast Message Stream ID (`broadcast` or `outbound`).
4. Click **'Test Connection'** to verify credentials before saving.
