## The AI Copilot Studio

Located under `/dashboard/ai`, the AI Marketing Studio allows you to generate content across three primary formats:

1. **Subject Lines**: Punchy, high-open-rate subject ideas.
2. **Social Captions**: Optimized for Twitter, LinkedIn, and Instagram with hashtags.
3. **Email Bodies**: Full promotional announcements, newsletter blurbs, and product launch letters.

## Tuning AI Parameters

Customize tone (Professional, Conversational, Urgent, Friendly), target audience description, and temperature to generate 3 distinct creative variations simultaneously.
