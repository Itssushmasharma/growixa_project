## Understanding Your Metrics

- **Sent**: Total recipients queued and dispatched.
- **Delivered**: Messages confirmed received by destination mail servers.
- **Opens**: Total and unique open counts tracked via invisible tracking pixel.
- **Clicks**: Unique link clicks across email body CTAs.
- **Bounces**: Hard bounces (invalid mailbox) and soft bounces (mailbox full / temporary reject).
