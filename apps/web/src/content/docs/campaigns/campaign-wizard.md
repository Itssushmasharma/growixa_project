## The 5 Steps Explained

1. **Campaign Setup**: Specify campaign name, from-address, reply-to, and subject line. Use **'✨ Ask AI'** to generate high-performing variations.
2. **Audience Selection**: Target your entire audience, specific contact lists, or targeted dynamic segments.
3. **Template & Content**: Choose responsive email templates or edit rich HTML copy.
4. **AI Optimization**: Fine-tune tone, urgency, and reading level.
5. **Review & Schedule**: Send a test email to your inbox, then choose immediate send or schedule for a future date.
