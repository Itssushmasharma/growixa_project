## Contact Status Breakdown

- **Active**: Subscribed contacts eligible to receive marketing and broadcast campaigns. Only active contacts count against your account contact quota (`max_contacts`).
- **Archived**: Inactive contacts excluded from regular campaign dispatches while preserving historical campaign delivery metrics.
- **Suppressed**: Permanently blocked from receiving campaigns due to unsubscribes, spam complaints, or hard bounces.

## Archiving vs Managing Contacts

Archiving allows you to remove inactive subscribers from your active campaigns and quota without losing past analytics and campaign engagement history.
