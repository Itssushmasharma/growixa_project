## Slide 2: The Problem
**The Martech Stack is Broken, Bloated, and Disconnected**
* **Tool Fragmentation:** Growing businesses use 5+ separate tools for CRM, copywriting, email delivery, social scheduling, and analytics.
* **The Copy-Paste AI Gap:** 80% of marketers use ChatGPT/Claude in silos—copying and pasting drafts into legacy tools without audience context.
* **The Trust & Reputation Risk:** Autonomous AI tools hallucinate and send low-converting spam, risking sender domain reputation and legal compliance (GDPR/CAN-SPAM).
