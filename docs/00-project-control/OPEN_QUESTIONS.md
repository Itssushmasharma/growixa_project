| OQ-025 | **Is Growixa building a pooled, Growixa-owned lead dataset (Layer B), or only real-time provider pass-through (Layer A)?** Layer B is a different business, not a bigger feature: a pool of personal data about people Growixa has no relationship with meets the working definition of a data broker, requiring registration in California, Texas, Vermont and Oregon (annual fees; California's Delete Act adds DROP, through which consumers delete across all registered brokers at once). It also requires **redistribution** rights from every source, which most directory and provider terms do not grant — several prohibit building a competing dataset outright, which is what a pool is. The ~35k already collected cannot seed it. A legitimate version exists: company-level records only, from redistribution-cleared sources. **Recommend Layer A for V1 and deferring Layer B until it is a deliberate business decision with counsel involved.** | `DEC-GRX-033` approval; Lead Intelligence V1 scope | OPEN |
| OQ-026 | **Does Growixa amend `DEC-GRX-006` to permit sequence-level (rather than per-message) human approval for automated outbound?** The Lead Intelligence vision includes AI analyzing a prospect, generating a personalized message, sending it, and following up automatically until conversion. As described that is autonomous sending, which `GRX-AI-002`/`GRX-AI-003`, `DEC-GRX-006` and `DEC-GRX-012` all prohibit — and which `PRD.md` §11 lists as **not a goal of Growixa at all, at any stage**, i.e. rejected rather than deferred. A narrower amendment delivers most of the value without reversing that position: AI drafts the entire sequence, a human approves the sequence once, execution proceeds against the approved plan. **Fully autonomous outbound is not proposed and would require amending PRD §11 itself.** | Lead Intelligence Layer C; any automated follow-up feature | OPEN |
| OQ-027 | **Who validates the per-jurisdiction sending-policy table before launch?** Lead Intelligence is scoped to global business (confirmed 2026-08-16), so lawfulness of contacting a stranger varies per recipient jurisdiction — permissive-with-conditions in the US, effectively opt-in under Canada's CASL, lawful-basis-plus-Art.14-notice under GDPR, and so on. Growixa would be asserting per-country lawfulness as a product behaviour. **This needs external counsel, not an agent's reading of secondary sources** — every jurisdictional claim recorded in `DEC-GRX-033` and `THREAT_MODEL.md` is a research starting point for that review, not a cleared position. | Lead Intelligence launch in any jurisdiction-sensitive channel | OPEN |
| OQ-024 | **Per-sending-path eligibility: what are the rules for `CUSTOM_SMTP` versus Postmark?** On Postmark, `OQ-020` closes the path for acquired contacts outright. On `CUSTOM_SMTP` the customer sends through their own server or ESP account, so Growixa is not the sender and Postmark's AUP is irrelevant — the constraints become (a) whatever the customer's own provider's AUP says, almost always an equivalent permission requirement, (b) the applicable law for the recipient's jurisdiction, and (c) Growixa's own terms of service. **Critically, `THREAT_MODEL.md` T83's shared-reputation blast radius disappears on this path** — the customer sends from their own domain and IP, so a spam trap harms them, not other Growixa customers. That makes `CUSTOM_SMTP` the honest home for externally-sourced sending. Open: does Growixa permit acquired-contact sends on `CUSTOM_SMTP` with a customer attestation of lawful basis, block them platform-wide regardless of path, or gate on jurisdiction? | `DEC-GRX-033`; channel eligibility design | OPEN |

Subsequent product discussion (2026-08-16) proposes the opposite: a Growixa-operated `LeadSourceProvider` discovery engine crawling company websites and official open datasets. Both are defensible and they are not compatible — the first is reversible, the second is much harder to walk back once it is Growixa's positioning. **Must be decided explicitly, not settled by whichever gets built first.** | `DEC-GRX-033` approval; Lead Intelligence module scope | OPEN |
